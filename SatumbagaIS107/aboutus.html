Kuliatan Marine Sanctuary: San Joaquin's Hidden Coastal Paradise — Philippines Along the southern tip of Iloilo province, where coastal hills meet the deep blue waters of the Panay Gulf, lies a coastal haven that stands as a shimmering testament to community-led marine conservation. Kuliatan Marine Sanctuary in Barangay Sinugbuhan offers travelers an immersive escape into biodiversity, rustic tranquility, and pristine aquatic life.


A Thriving Marine World

For generations, the surrounding marine waters served as primary sustenance for local fisherfolk.
